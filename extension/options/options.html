StreamRatings setup

One step: add your free OMDb key and IMDb ratings appear on Netflix, Prime Video and JioHotstar.

  1. Get a free OMDb key (choose FREE, then click the link in the email OMDb sends you).
  2. Paste the key below and click Save.
Stored only in your browser. Free keys allow 1,000 lookups a day; ratings are shared between all StreamRatings users, so most titles won't use your quota at all.

Cache

Ratings are cached and refreshed every 3–30 days depending on how recent the title is.
